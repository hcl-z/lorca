Lorca's user docs are on its website, as Markdown:

- https://lorca.app/llms.txt lists every page by section, with a line on what each covers. Start here.
- https://lorca.app/docs/<page>.md is one page, such as https://lorca.app/docs/routines.md. A link in the list or in a page, such as /docs/routines#timezones, names that page.
- https://lorca.app/llms-full.txt is every page in one file, to search across them.
- The Simplified Chinese pages are under /zh: https://lorca.app/zh/llms.txt and https://lorca.app/zh/docs/<page>.md.

When the user asks what Lorca can do, how to do something in it, or why it behaves as it does, read the list, then the pages that cover it, and answer from them, with the steps and the names of the menus and settings they give. Fetch them with your web fetch tool when you have one, else with `curl -fsSL <url>`. Do not answer from memory, and do not read Lorca's binary, app bundle, or data files to find out how it works.

The website describes the newest Lorca. When the user's app lacks something a page describes, an update brings it.
