# Questions with options

A bot asks the user to choose with `ask_user` (`crates/cli/src/ask.rs`): a question, two to six options with an optional one-line description each (checked by the tool, not the schema, so a call with too few is told to list them or to ask in its reply instead), `multiple` for several picks, `allow_other` for a line of the user's own words, and `recommended`, the place of the option the bot suggests. A turn nobody watches (a routine, a workflow's sample) does not get to ask; the tool tells the bot to decide and say what it chose.

## The card and the wait

The question is a permission card (`Body::Permission`) with `tool` `ask`, the question as its `summary`, and an `ask` of `AskQuestion { question, options[{ label, description? }], multiple?, allow_other?, recommended?, answer? }`. The call waits as every card does (`mcp::await_answer`): ten minutes at most, Stop ends it, and a message the user writes instead dismisses it, so the bot follows that message. A pending question is pushed to the phones with the question as its text.

## The answer

The apps answer through `chats.permission { chat_id, message_id, decision, answer? }`, which goes to the bot's Runner here or sealed, as any card's answer does. `decision` `allow` carries `answer { choices: [place], other? }`; `deny` skips the question. On the Runner `ask::answer` checks it against the card: picks within the options, one pick unless `multiple`, the user's own words only with `allow_other` and, for one pick, instead of an option. A wrong answer is refused and the card keeps waiting. A good one is kept on the card as `ask.answer`, so every Device shows what was picked, then the waiting call returns it: "The user answered: "Thorough"", with the answer in its details.

| Outcome | Card `decision` | The bot hears |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Picked | `allowed`, with `ask.answer` | the labels picked and the user's words |
| Skip | `denied` | to go on with what it thinks best and say what it chose |
| Stop | `dismissed` | that the turn was stopped |
| A new message | `dismissed` | to follow that message; the turn ends after this batch |
| Ten minutes | `expired` | to say what it needs decided and stop |

## In the apps

- **Desktop** (`desktop/ask_card.go`, `model/ask.go`): each option is a bordered row with a radio for one pick or a checkbox for several, its description under it, Recommended on the bot's pick, and its number, which 1 to 9 pick while the options have the keyboard. One pick sends on its click; several, or Other, which opens a field, send with Submit. Skip leaves the choice to the bot. The card reads the answer at once (`Store.AnswerAsk`) and goes back to asking, with why under it, when the Runner refuses or cannot be reached. Answered, it keeps the question and says "You chose …", or Skipped, No answer in time, or Answered in the chat instead.
- **The phone** (`QuestionRow` in `src/ui/transcript.tsx`, `src/core/ask.ts`) draws the same card in its transcript style: the question, then the options on a white card, each a row with its number, description, and Recommended, a radio for one pick or a box for several, and Other… with a field where the question takes the user's own words. One pick sends on its tap; several, or Other, send with Submit; Skip sits beside it. `ask.ts` checks the answer as the Runner does before it goes. A refusal puts the card back with why under it; answered, it says "You chose …".

## A coding agent's questions

A [coding agent](coding-agents.md#its-questions) a bot runs asks with a form, which `crates/cli/src/coding/mod.rs` puts in the chat as the bot's card.

The question is a card of its own in the chat, the bot's (`converse`): `Body::Permission` with `tool` `agent_ask`, the summary "Claude Code asks: Which database?", and an `agent_ask` of `AgentAsk { agent_id, kind, name, row_id, message, fields: [{ key, label, kind: one | many | flag | text | number | integer, required?, choices?: [{ value, title, description? }], other? }], answers?, by? }`. One waits at a time per agent, with the permission questions. It counts as unread and pushes as a permission card does, the agent's card says `asking` and names it (`asking`), and the record keeps it (`ask_card`), so a restart closes it.

The user answers the whole form from any Device with `coding.answer { chat_id, message_id: row_id, values }`, by field key, or declines it with `decline: true`; the request goes through the agent's row as its other requests do. `answer_form` fills the form (`fill`): a field by its key or its label, a pick by its value or its title in any case, the user's own words where the field takes them (for a menu, in its free-text field), yes or no, a number that parses; what the form does not ask, a pick it does not offer, or a required field left out is refused with why, and the question keeps waiting. The card shows the answer at once, `decision` `allowed` with `answers` (each field's label and its answer in words) and `by` (`you` or `bot`), then the agent gets `accept` and the content. Declined, the card is `denied` and the agent hears `decline`. Nobody answering in ten minutes makes the card `expired` and the agent hears `cancel`; an agent that stops or a Lorca that quits first makes it `dismissed`.

The bot hears the question when the agent is one nobody watches (`Record.unattended`), or when the user made it their proxy (`Record.proxy`): it puts the `Asked` news on the agent and wakes the bot as a finished agent does, with the message and each field, its label and what it takes, choices numbered (`ask_fields`). An attended agent's question waits on the agent's card for the user and the bot is not woken; `coding_agent answer` is refused for it unless the user made the bot their proxy, so no other turn answers it either. `coding_agent answer { id, answers }` answers by field label (a choice's title or number from 1, a list where it takes several, text, true or false, a number); `choice` (from 1; yes is 1, no 2) or `text` answers a form that asks one thing (`bot_answers`); `decline: true` declines. The bot is told to answer only what the user's own words settle; whoever answers first wins, the card says who, and an answer by the bot is a line in the transcript. A question answered or stopped takes the news back.

The question's card (`desktop/agent_ask_card.go`; the phone's `AgentAskRow` in `src/ui/transcript.tsx` with `src/core/agentAsk.ts`, in the card and in the `AgentAskSheet` bottom sheet) leads with the agent's mark on a tile in its color, "Claude Code asks", and that the bot or the user may answer, then its message as Markdown, then each field under its label ("(optional)" where it may be left out): picks as bordered rows with a radio, or a checkbox where it takes several, each with its description, and Other with a field for the user's own words where the field takes them; yes or no as two such rows; text and numbers as fields. A form of one pick sends on its click, as the bot's own questions do (above), with 1 to 9 picking on the desktop; the rest send with Submit once every required field has an answer. Decline sits beside it. The desktop reads the answer at once (`Store.AnswerAgentAsk`) and goes back to asking, with why, when the Runner refuses. On the phone the card keeps an Answer button that opens the same form in a bottom sheet (the `QuestionSheet` in `src/ui/DetailSheets.tsx`), so it can be answered without scrolling the transcript; the form is the same in both. Answered, the card keeps the question and shows the answer as a reply beside a green bar, each field's label small over its answer (no label for a form of one field), with "Answered by Developer" under it when the bot answered; declined, unanswered in time, or stopped first, it says so in a line of its own. The desktop's full-screen agent view shows the same card under the transcript.
