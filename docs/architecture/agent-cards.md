# Coding agent cards

How the apps draw a [coding agent](coding-agents.md) a bot supervises: the card in the chat, its transcript, the desktop's running-agents capsule, and the phone's sheet.

How an agent stands is read off its card once, in the CLI (`AgentRun::summarize`, on every card it stores and sends the apps, one from before included): its `tone` (`ask` while it needs the user or went quiet while working, `busy` while it is checked, starts, or works, `done` when idle, `fail`, or `quiet` once it ended otherwise), `busy` (it starts or works and asks nothing, so its dot breathes and its time ticks), `context` (the tokens used of its window, from its `Context` setting), `now` (its last tool's line, without the mark), `status` (the key of its pill's words: `starting`, `needs_you`, `working`, `quiet`, `done`, `ended`, `failed`, `held`, `not_allowed`, `no_answer`, `dismissed`, or `stopped`, which each app translates), `model` (its model's name without a gateway's prefix and context hints, the choice in force's name where it can change), `pull_request_name` (`acme/shop #7` for a pull request's link), and `tail` (its last five lines with something on them, while it starts or works and asks nothing, which the consoles show). The apps tint by the tone and read the rest; they keep no rules of their own for it.

The `coding_agent` row that started an agent carries its card from the start, `Body::Tool.agent`: `{ id, kind, name?, settings?: [{ name, value }], controls?: [{ what: model | effort, name, value, choices: [{ value, name }] }], task, folder, branch?, state, stalled, held, question?: { kind: start | command, command?, reason?, rule? }, asking?, title?, output?, outcome?, pull_request?, changes?: { files, added, removed }, device?, started_at? }`, with `checking` while Auto-review judges the start. `output` is its last 20 lines. The card keeps its first place in the relay's log however often it changes, a question on it counts as unread and pushes as a command's does ([Notifications](notifications.md)), and it stays after the agent ended. `coding.transcript` (its last 2,000 lines and its card), `coding.stop`, and `coding.answer` go to the bot's Runner from any Device as sealed requests.

Every app shows the card in the chat: the agent's name, with Stop at the end of the line while it runs; what it was asked; how it stands in a word (Starting, Working, Quiet, Done, Ended, Failed, Stopped) with its branch; and while it works its last lines. Before it starts the title is the bot's ("Developer wants to start Claude Code on Workbench") with Allow once, Always allow, and Deny; for a command, "Claude Code wants to run a command" with the command in a code block that opens it whole. While the agent's own question waits, the status reads "Asks you in the chat".

The question's card is drawn as [Questions](questions.md#a-coding-agents-questions) describes. A click on what it works on opens its transcript, which asks for what changed every two seconds on the Runner and five elsewhere, and keeps to its end unless the user scrolled up.

The desktop draws every agent surface from one kit (`desktop/agentkit.go`): the agent's mark (`agentmarks.go`, Claude's spark, OpenAI's knot for Codex, a prompt for the rest) on a tile in its color; how it stands as a pill in its state's tint, whose dot breathes while it works, with how long it has worked (`started_at`); a console, a dark well in both appearances with the last lines, each tool's line bright and a spinner on the one it runs now; and callouts for what it asks and how it failed. The card (`desktop/agents.go`) is the tile, the name over its branch and Runner, the pill, and Stop; what it was asked; while it works, the console; its session as a strip of cells, the model (its `model`), the effort, and the mode, each a label over its value and a pop-up where it can change now (`controls`, `coding.set { chat_id, message_id, what, value }`), over a meter of its context with its share and what it cost; the pull request it opened; and a footer with when it started and a link to the transcript. The transcript (`desktop/agenttranscript.go`) is a page of steps, as Cursor draws an agent's run: its header is the agent's mark, what it was asked (its session's title, else the task), its name, how it stands, and where it works, with its pull request and Stop; each step is a line below it. What it was sent is a card that says who wrote it, the user in the accent's tint or the bot, folding a long brief behind Show more; a thought is one line, "Thought for 3s", that opens to what it thought; a tool is one line in the past or present tense ("Ran bun test", "Editing src/a.rs"), and a run of reads and searches folds into one line ("Explored 3 files, 2 searches") that opens to each; what it said is Markdown; the plan is a checklist; its notices, refusals, and compactions are lines of their own. A failed command reads "Failed: …" in red. A click opens or closes a step; a tool that runs, one that failed, and the last one are open, with its output and its edits, added lines green and removed ones red, under it. A thought and a compaction open to their text, and the plan counts its steps done. An open command's output is on MyGo's terminal plugin (`desktop/agentterm.go`), the latest six at most, each a read-only terminal with no program that is fed what the output adds after what it has, its colors only (`terminalBytes` drops the rest again), so nothing an agent's command printed drives the emulator; an older one, and any where the plugin's library cannot load, is plain text. An edit is its diff, added lines green and removed ones red. The footer is what its work changed, "+12 −3 in 4 files", and its branch; the sheet's Done closes it, and the full view keeps its column of facts beside the same steps. The phone draws the same kit with native views (`src/ui/agentKit.tsx`, what it reads off the card in `src/core/agentKit.ts`): the card is `AgentRow` in `src/ui/transcript.tsx`, and a tap on it opens `app/agent/[id].tsx`, a sheet that is the phone's full view: the header with Stop; what the desktop's column beside the transcript holds, stacked above it; the timeline of the same entries in a FlashList, the overview its header and what the agent asks its footer, so a run of hundreds of entries draws those near the screen (`src/core/agentEntries.ts`, read again every five seconds while the sheet is up, where an entry at the revision it holds keeps its row as drawn), a command's output in its colors (`outputSpans`, which keeps `ESC [ … m` only); and what the agent asks under it.

On the desktop the agents at work sit in the content header's top right as a capsule (`desktop/runningagents.go`): their tiles stacked, newest first, with a count past three. A click opens a custom popover under it, one row an agent with its tile, name, pill, and what it was asked; a row opens that agent's transcript sheet. The floating windows and their full view are gone. On the phone the same capsule sits at the trailing end of the chat bar (`src/ui/RunningAgentsMenu.tsx`): the tiles stacked and a count past three; a tap opens a custom dropdown under the bar listing every agent, and a row opens its sheet (`app/agent/[id].tsx`). The cards over the composer are gone. The sheet follows the desktop transcript: a prompt card that folds to two lines behind a chevron, a thought line with how long it took, tool lines in the past or present tense with reads and searches folded into one, the plan, and the changes its work made; its session is the card's `SpecStrip`. While the agent runs, the card and the full view show its model and effort as pop-ups (menus on the phone) (`controls`, which the driver reads from the session's `configOptions`) that ask the Runner to change them (`coding.set { chat_id, message_id, what, value }`); a card that asks shows none.
