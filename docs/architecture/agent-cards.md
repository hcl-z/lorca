# Coding agent cards

How the apps draw a [coding agent](coding-agents.md) a bot supervises: the card in the chat, its transcript, the desktop's floating window, and the phone's sheet.

How an agent stands is read off its card once, in the CLI (`AgentRun::summarize`, on every card it stores and sends the apps, one from before included): its `tone` (`ask` while it needs the user or went quiet while working, `busy` while it is checked, starts, or works, `done` when idle, `fail`, or `quiet` once it ended otherwise), `busy` (it starts or works and asks nothing, so its dot breathes and its time ticks), `context` (the tokens used of its window, from its `Context` setting), `now` (its last tool's line, without the mark), `status` (the key of its pill's words: `starting`, `needs_you`, `working`, `quiet`, `done`, `ended`, `failed`, `held`, `not_allowed`, `no_answer`, `dismissed`, or `stopped`, which each app translates), `model` (its model's name without a gateway's prefix and context hints, the choice in force's name where it can change), `pull_request_name` (`acme/shop #7` for a pull request's link), and `tail` (its last five lines with something on them, while it starts or works and asks nothing, which the consoles show). The apps tint by the tone and read the rest; they keep no rules of their own for it.

The `coding_agent` row that started an agent carries its card from the start, `Body::Tool.agent`: `{ id, kind, name?, settings?: [{ name, value }], controls?: [{ what: model | effort, name, value, choices: [{ value, name }] }], task, folder, branch?, state, stalled, held, question?: { kind: start | command, command?, reason?, rule? }, asking?, title?, output?, outcome?, pull_request?, device?, started_at? }`, with `checking` while Auto-review judges the start. `output` is its last 20 lines. The card keeps its first place in the relay's log however often it changes, a question on it counts as unread and pushes as a command's does ([Notifications](notifications.md)), and it stays after the agent ended. `coding.transcript` (its last 2,000 lines), `coding.stop`, and `coding.answer` go to the bot's Runner from any Device as sealed requests.

Every app shows the card in the chat: the agent's name, with Stop at the end of the line while it runs; what it was asked; how it stands in a word (Starting, Working, Quiet, Done, Ended, Failed, Stopped) with its branch; and while it works its last lines. Before it starts the title is the bot's ("Developer wants to start Claude Code on Workbench") with Allow once, Always allow, and Deny; for a command, "Claude Code wants to run a command" with the command in a code block that opens it whole. While the agent's own question waits, the status reads "Asks you in the chat".

The question's card is drawn as [Questions](questions.md#a-coding-agents-questions) describes. A click on what it works on opens its transcript, which asks for what changed every two seconds on the Runner and five elsewhere, and keeps to its end unless the user scrolled up.

The desktop draws every agent surface from one kit (`desktop/agentkit.go`): the agent's mark (`agentmarks.go`, Claude's spark, OpenAI's knot for Codex, a prompt for the rest) on a tile in its color; how it stands as a pill in its state's tint, whose dot breathes while it works, with how long it has worked (`started_at`); a console, a dark well in both appearances with the last lines, each tool's line bright and a spinner on the one it runs now; and callouts for what it asks and how it failed. The card (`desktop/agents.go`) is the tile, the name over its branch and Runner, the pill, and Stop; what it was asked; while it works, the console; its session as a strip of cells, the model (its `model`), the effort, and the mode, each a label over its value and a pop-up where it can change now (`controls`, `coding.set { chat_id, message_id, what, value }`), over a meter of its context with its share and what it cost; the pull request it opened; and a footer with when it started and a link to the transcript. The transcript (`desktop/agenttranscript.go`) is a timeline: each turn opens with what the bot sent, in the accent, and each entry sits on a rail with a node of its own, the agent's tile for what it said (Markdown), a tool's state (a spinner, a mark of its kind, a red cross), a thought, the plan, a compaction, or a notice in its level's tint. A tool's line shows its edits' added and removed lines and a failed command's exit code, and a click opens or closes it; a tool that runs, one that failed, and the last one are open. A thought and a compaction open to their text, and the plan counts its steps done. An open command's output is on MyGo's terminal plugin (`desktop/agentterm.go`), the latest six at most, each a read-only terminal with no program that is fed what the output adds after what it has, its colors only (`terminalBytes` drops the rest again), so nothing an agent's command printed drives the emulator; an older one, and any where the plugin's library cannot load, is plain text. An edit is its diff, added lines green and removed ones red. The phone draws the same kit with native views (`src/ui/agentKit.tsx`, what it reads off the card in `src/core/agentKit.ts`): the card is `AgentRow` in `src/ui/transcript.tsx`, and a tap on it opens `app/agent/[id].tsx`, a sheet that is the phone's full view: the header with Stop; what the desktop's column beside the transcript holds, stacked above it; the timeline of the same entries in a FlashList, the overview its header and what the agent asks its footer, so a run of hundreds of entries draws those near the screen (`src/core/agentEntries.ts`, read again every five seconds while the sheet is up, where an entry at the revision it holds keeps its row as drawn), a command's output in its colors (`outputSpans`, which keeps `ESC [ … m` only); and what the agent asks under it.

On the desktop every agent at work also has a window of its own that floats over whatever is on screen, a chat or not (`desktop/agentpip.go`; `Store.RunningAgents` lists them from the latest 80 messages of each chat). It comes up when the agent starts working, the next ones a little off the first, and goes when the agent is done or stopped. Its title drags it, kept inside the app window: the tile, the name, the pill, Full Screen, and Close; its body is the card, smaller: what it works on, its console, its model and effort with Stop, and what it asks, answered there as on the card, with a hairline along its foot of how full its context is. Close keeps it away until the agent works again. A click on its body, or Full Screen, fills the app window with the agent: a header with its tile, its name over its session's title, its pill, and Stop; its transcript in a reading column 760 wide, with its question's card under it; and a column beside it of what it works on, its workspace, its session, and its plan with its steps done; Escape or the button in the corner brings the window back. On the phone the agents at work in every chat sit over the composer as small cards on its floating glass (`src/ui/RunningAgents.tsx`), the latest started first and sideways when there are several: the tile, the name, the pill, what it does now or what it asks, Stop, Close, and the context hairline; a tap opens its sheet from whichever chat is open. While the agent runs, the card and the full view show its model and effort as pop-ups (menus on the phone) (`controls`, which the driver reads from the session's `configOptions`) that ask the Runner to change them (`coding.set { chat_id, message_id, what, value }`); a card that asks shows none.
