# Phone app look and layout

The screens of the [phone app](phone-app.md) (`mobile/`): its palette and bars, the chat list and the composer, forms and sheets, the transcript and its cards, and a tablet's two panes.

The UI is native, in the desktop app's monochrome look: neutral surfaces, ink (near-black in light, near-white in dark) for the user's bubble, the primary buttons, checkmarks, and switches, system blue only for links, and bots in their accents. Its bars float round buttons, glass discs that UIKit's bar draws on iOS and the app's own (`RoundButton` and `CapsuleButton` in `src/ui/chrome.tsx`: a white disc with a soft shadow) on Android. The sheets (`src/ui/Sheet.tsx`, on `@expo/ui/community/bottom-sheet`) are the native bottom sheets: participants, the composer's attachment sources, an agent's quick actions and question, a bot's question, a marker's whole message, a command, the chat's running tasks, the New menu, and the model picker. The routes behind the message, command, and tasks details stay for deep links; the chat opens the sheet and a task row dismisses it before pushing the command route. The full transcript and the file viewer stay full-screen.

The chat list has no title: a Settings disc (and Attention beside it while something waits) at the leading edge, Search and New at the trailing one, rows of a large avatar, the title with its stamp at the end of the line, a one-line preview, and an ink count while unread, with no chevrons or separators. The composer, after the reference's, is a round `+` button beside a pill (`Float` in `chrome.tsx`: glass where `expo-glass-effect` has it, filled elsewhere) floating over the transcript with its disc inside the pill's right edge (Dictate, Send, or Stop), riding the keyboard as a `KeyboardStickyView` while the transcript's `KeyboardChatScrollView` lifts the last messages with the keys by growing its bottom inset, so the list is never resized. The transcript is a `LegendList` (`@legendapp/list`) drawn in that scroll view: it opens at its end with short chats resting on the composer, follows the end while the reader is within a tenth of a screen of it, keeps the reader's place while an older page loads above (asked for as the top comes within a screen), and a sent message comes to rest under the header with space kept below it (`anchoredEndSpace`) that the reply fills as it lands; that space is the scroll view's `blankSpace`, the floor under the keyboard's lift, so a keyboard that fits in it lifts nothing. A disc to jump to the end floats over the composer while the end is more than 160 pt below; form sheets on iOS and full-screen Material modal screens on Android for chat info, new bot, new group, and settings; a Device's screen under Settings (`app/settings/device/[id].tsx`) with Rename… under its name, where a Runner whose CLI updates itself has a Lorca CLI row with its version and where its update stands ([Updates and the service](runtime.md#updates-and-the-service)), the row itself offering Update while a newer release is out and the Runner is online; provider credential screens under Settings, with API-key fields and an in-app browser for subscription OAuth, the same browser that opens a computer's plugin sign-in from its card, and custom providers after the built-in ones: Add Custom Provider is a native menu of the same presets as the desktop apps (one the account has is checked and opens it) and Other Server…, opening a form (`app/settings/custom-provider.tsx`: name, API menu, base URL with the URL the CLI will call and, for a server on localhost, a note to name it as the Runners reach it, key, a Models row, and a Default Model menu) whose Models row slides in a picker (`app/settings/custom-models.tsx`) of the server's listed models with a native search bar that offers Add "…" for an id it does not list; the two share their rows through `src/ui/modelDraft.ts`; Link previews and context menus on iOS chat rows, with swipe actions on an iPhone row as in Messages (Pin and Delete to the left, Mark as Read to the right while it has unread messages; one row open at a time, closed when the list scrolls), and Material long-press menus on Android; the desktop app's Lucide icons on both platforms, by the SF Symbol names the models use (`Symbol` in `src/ui/Symbol.tsx`, drawn by `react-native-svg` from `src/ui/lucideIcons.ts`, which `bun run phone-icons` writes from the desktop's `icons_lucide.go`), with the system's own icons in native bars and menus; a push button shared by the cards and screens (`Button` in `src/ui/Button.tsx`: an ink capsule for the leading action, a quiet fill with a hairline for the others, red words for a removal, an optional leading icon, sinking on a spring with a light haptic tap on iOS); one palette on both platforms, the desktop's tokens, built once per appearance (`src/ui/theme.ts`), and for `@expo/ui`'s Compose views a neutral Material scheme with ink as its primary, given once at the root; message text follows the Dynamic Type size on iOS (Android sizes it in sp); haptics by what happened (`src/ui/haptics.ts`): UIKit's feedback generators on iOS, the system haptic constants on Android, which follow its touch-feedback setting. Android presses ripple rather than highlight (`src/ui/Pressable.tsx`), alerts are Material 3 dialogs from Compose rather than AppCompat's (`src/ui/alert.tsx`, `Alert.alert`'s arguments; UIKit's alert on iOS), and the app theme is Material 3 in the same colors rather than the wallpaper's (`plugins/with-material-theme.js`), which Android's own text handles and overscroll take. Forms (`src/ui/forms.tsx`) are white cards with a hairline edge on the grouped background, and otherwise follow each platform's lists: iOS's uppercase section titles, disclosure chevrons, trailing checkmarks, and UIKit switches in ink; on Android, subheaders in ink, no chevrons, a radio button (or a checkbox where several can be picked) before a choice, Material 3's Compose switch with the whole row toggling it, and a dropdown arrow on a value menu. A photo opens in the file viewer and zooms by pinch or double tap: in a zooming UIScrollView on iOS, by gestures on the UI thread on Android. The chat list's New and the composer's `+` attachment sources open bottom sheets; a chat row's and a section header's long press go through `PopupMenu` (`src/ui/PopupMenu.tsx`): UIKit's menu on iOS, and on Android a rounded card in the palette that the app draws, opening beside the button or under the finger that pressed a row, growing out of that point, with submenus under a header that goes back and a scrim, a tap outside, or Back to close it. Android's other menus are Jetpack Compose views from `@expo/ui`. The floating discs and capsules take their shadow from `floatShadow` on a view that does not clip, since `overflow: "hidden"` removes an iOS shadow, and a dark surface on Android shows its hairline edge alone. The transcript and the chat list keep their rows drawn a screen past the viewport (`drawDistance`) and do not detach offscreen views, and Android's `MarkdownView` rebuilds its text views only when its Markdown or palette changed, so a recycled row is never empty for a frame. A row's Markdown is measured natively on the JS thread before it draws (`measureMarkdown`), once per text, width, and text size: the sizes are kept by a hash of those, without the text, in a file in the app's caches folder for the build, so the first chat opened after a launch measures only messages it has not drawn before. Search opens a capsule at the top of the list with Cancel beside it ([Sidebar](sidebar.md#in-the-apps)). Conversation headers overlay the transcript on both platforms; UIKit supplies its navigation material, while Android lays a nearly opaque veil in the surface color through the status bar and controls and fades it into the transcript, leaving scrolled messages faintly visible. Both bars hold Back and a capsule of the members and title (a tap opens Participants) at the start, and one disc at the end: Running tasks while there are any, else ⋯ for Details. The transcript follows the macOS app: the user's bubbles in ink, the bots' in light gray, with the bot's name above and its avatar beside the bubble's bottom edge in a group, neither in a DM; a message taller than a folded bubble shows, about 320 pt, lays out only its head, cut at a line and fading into the bubble's fill with Show more (`FoldedText` in `src/ui/FoldedText.tsx`), and a tap grows it into a page of its own from the bubble's place and closes back (`MessageReader.tsx`), a message still streaming folding the same head so each flush lays out the fold, not the whole reply; "Today 4:13 AM" separators after fifteen minutes; the "is working" row ("Working…" in a DM, "Chef is working…" in a group, or what the one working bot is doing, in the Mac's words and by its rules, such as "Thinking…" or "Running command: Install dependencies…" (`workingActivity` in `src/ui/format.ts`), its words shimmering inside `ShimmerView`, a native container in `lorca-core` that masks its children with the Mac's sweeping gradient) and the breathing green dot on avatars; "Message from ◉ X" and "Messaged ◉ X" markers; tool calls never shown, except a `bash` call's command card while the command needs the user, after the Mac's (`showsCard` in `src/core/model.ts`): the command's first line in a code block that opens the whole command in a bottom sheet on tap (`CommandSheet` in `src/ui/DetailSheets.tsx`; the `app/command/[id].tsx` route remains for deep links); while Auto-review asks, its question with the answers; once the bot handed the running command over, Stop on the title's line and its last lines in a code block that scrolls past six lines, fading at an edge with more past it (Android's `fadingEdgeLength`, a gradient in the block's color on iOS); at a question, Answer, which opens `@expo/ui`'s bottom sheet sized to its content (SwiftUI's on iOS, Material's on Android; `src/ui/AnswerSheet.tsx`) holding only a field, with the command's question as its placeholder, that takes the text as typed, without capitalization or correction, and hides it unless the question is a yes or no, and Send; the sheet closes when the command ends; a coding agent's card from the start, its transcript a sheet away (`app/agent/[id].tsx`; [Coding agents](agent-cards.md)); Running tasks (see [Terminal sessions](terminal-sessions.md)) as a terminal button beside the chat's ⋯ (a native bar button on iOS, the Android header's own button), which opens them as rows in a bottom sheet (`TasksSheet` in `src/ui/DetailSheets.tsx`; the route under `app/tasks/` remains for deep links), each opening its command's details: the bot and how the command stands, Run in Background while the bot's call waits on it, Stop Command, the whole command, and its last lines; sidebar-style previews and stamps, and in the chat list, while a chat has a turn in flight, three dots lighting up in turn and "Working…" in place of its preview.

A window at least 700 pt wide (a tablet, or a tablet window with room to spare) shows two panes (`app/(main)/_layout.tsx`, `src/ui/layout.tsx`): the chat list as a sidebar (320 pt, 375 pt from 1100 pt) and the open chat beside it; a narrower one stacks the chat over the list. The route group `(main)` holds the list and the chat in a stack of their own inside the root stack, so sheets and the pairing screen cover both panes. The sidebar (`src/ui/Sidebar.tsx`) is the same `ChatsScreen` in a native stack of its own, one screen deep, in an independent navigation tree, so it keeps the native bar and toolbar buttons; the pane's stack starts at an empty "Select a chat" screen, a chat picked in the sidebar replaces the one open, its row stays lit, and the chat's bar has no back button. Sidebar rows open their menu on a long press on both platforms, with no Link preview. On iOS the sidebar sits in a `CompactWidthView` (`modules/lorca-core`), a container that overrides the horizontal size class to compact as a split view's sidebar column does. The sidebar searches as the phone's list does; on Android its bar is the list's own rather than an item of the activity's one action bar, which react-native-screens hands to whichever stack updated its header last, so a sidebar's item would vanish once the pane opens a chat. The transcript sits in a `SoftScrollEdgeView`, which gives its scroll view the soft top edge a phone has where an iPad would rule a line under the bar. Bubbles and Markdown measure against the pane's width (`usePaneWidth`) and stop growing at 620 pt. The list's sections, its Hidden group, and its Mute and Move to Section menus are in [Sidebar sections and alerts](sidebar.md#in-the-apps).

A bot's [question with options](questions.md#in-the-apps) is its own card (`QuestionRow`, `tool` `ask`): the options to tap, Other…, Submit, and Skip, and once answered on any Device what was picked.

The transcript's cards (a permission, a question, a secret request, a command, a coding agent, a draft) are gray panels like the bots' bubbles, holding what they show on a white card inside, with their choices as equal buttons, the first in ink (`CardButton`); a draft's card says who drafted it over the account, To, the subject, and the text, with Send email (or Send) and Discard. Under the latest exchange of a direct chat sit Copy and Regenerate (under the bot's reply) and Edit (under the user's message), in [Running a reply again](runtime.md#running-a-reply-again). Web links in a bot's message are [source chips](links.md#on-the-phone).
